Helpful information before you enquire about an RO purifier or request service.

How do I choose the right RO purifier?

It depends on your water source and water quality. If possible, check the water TDS and other relevant parameters, then compare purifier specifications. Contact us with your area and water source for guidance; confirm the exact model before buying.

Are the displayed prices final?

The website shows indicative discounted price tiers based on the prices provided. Confirm the exact product, availability, installation, delivery and final payable amount with Hydro Plus before ordering.

Can I book an RO repair?

Yes. Use the contact form or WhatsApp and share the purifier brand, model, location and symptoms. Service availability and charges will be confirmed before a visit.

Do you provide installation?

You can request installation assistance. Share the product model and location so the team can confirm coverage, timing and charges.

Can you replace filters and membranes?

Send your purifier brand and model. The correct compatible part, price and service availability need to be checked before replacement.
